# Voice And Format

## Editorial Lens

This workflow is not "AI productivity tips". It is a soft, precise observation of how AI changes work while young people try to keep an intact inner life.

Use this order of attention:

1. Feeling
2. Inner conflict
3. Work reality
4. AI context
5. Small personal action

## Audience

- 20-30 year-old small-town youth entering city work, platform work, or unstable careers.
- White-collar workers with good education but thin meaning, high self-demand, and AI anxiety.
- People who want clarity and companionship more than slogans.

## Tone

Use:

- 温和
- 敏感
- 清醒
- 不催促
- 低声但有穿透力
- 把模糊感受说清楚

Avoid:

- "逆袭", "翻身", "搞钱", "认知升级", "人设爆破"
- "你必须", "普通人唯一机会", "再不学就晚了"
- Treating AI as a god, monster, or magic shortcut
- Copying any creator's exact phrases

## Source Verification Fields

Each source should be reduced into:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| title | Video/interview/report title |
| speaker | Speaker, creator, or account |
| platform | Douyin, podcast, media, official site, etc. |
| url | Traceable link |
| published_at | Exact date if available; otherwise original raw time and status |
| quote_summary | Concise summary of original speech, not long quotation |
| relevance | Why it matters for AI/work/individual value |

If publish time is missing or vague, mark it as "待补发布时间". Do not silently treat it as verified.

## Scoring

Score every topic out of 100:

- Propagation potential, 50 points
  - Clear tension: 15
  - Shareable title: 15
  - Timely source support: 10
  - Series potential: 10
- Emotional resonance, 50 points
  - Names a real feeling: 20
  - Gentle rather than scolding: 10
  - Offers inner permission: 10
  - Has a small life-level landing point: 10

Choose the highest score as the draft topic unless the user asks otherwise.

## Default Topic Families

Use these only as creative scaffolds, not fixed titles:

1. AI becomes stronger; humans need to recover feeling.
2. Before treating yourself as an AI-native company, stop treating yourself as a machine.
3. Work gets lighter, but the heart gets more tired.
4. The rare ability is no longer execution, but sensing what is worth doing.
5. Individual value begins when you stop outsourcing your judgment.

## 5-Minute Draft Structure

Target length: 900-1200 Chinese characters.

Use these sections:

```text
[开场]
15-25 seconds. One gentle hook, no panic.

[来源引入]
30 seconds. Mention 1-2 recent verified sources and their original-speech summaries.

[情绪翻译]
About 1 minute. Translate the public issue into the listener's private feeling.

[观点展开]
About 2 minutes. One main idea, no more than three subpoints.

[生活落点]
About 1 minute. A small action: record feelings, notice discomfort, revise rhythm, ask a better question.

[结尾]
30 seconds. Leave one question, not a command.
```

## Output Package

Use this order:

1. Source verification
2. Topic options
3. Best topic
4. 5-minute draft
5. Title alternatives
6. Release intro
7. Comment prompt
8. Next direction
